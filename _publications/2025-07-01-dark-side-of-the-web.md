We analysed 6.6M forum posts, 3.4M chat messages and 120,000 darknet websites to compare these
sources for cyber threat intelligence. My part was the NLP pipeline: HTML text extraction, language
detection, cybersecurity terminology matching, GPU-accelerated BERTopic topic modelling and LLaMA-2
topic labelling, parallelised on ETH's HPC cluster.
