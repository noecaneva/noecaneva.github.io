Master's thesis at the Seminar for Applied Mathematics, ETH Zürich, with the research done at ICSI /
UC Berkeley in the Mahoney Group. Supervised by Prof. Michael Mahoney, Prof. Siddhartha Mishra,
Dr. Kareem Hegazy and Dr. Jose Antonio Lara Benitez.

- Extended the NeurDE framework to the shallow water equations and magnetohydrodynamics
- Implemented Zhou and Dellar polynomial equilibria, a spectral Orszag–Tang reference solver, and KBC
  entropic stabilization
- Derived an entropically consistent KBC equilibrium via constrained minimization
