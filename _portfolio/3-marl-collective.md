Bachelor's thesis at CSElab, supervised by Prof. Petros Koumoutsakos, Daniel Wälchli and Pascal Weber,
done during a research stay at Harvard University. Reproduced swarming, milling and schooling from
three behavioural rules, then used multi-agent RL (V-RACER) to reproduce milling. Some of the results were
used in Dr. Daniel Wälchli's PhD dissertation.
