I work at the intersection of scientific machine learning, AI safety and security. I hold an MSc in
Computational Science and Engineering from ETH Zürich (coursework and thesis complete, degree
conferred February 2027), and I did my master's thesis as a Visiting Student Researcher at UC Berkeley
and ICSI in the Mahoney Group.

My thesis, *Neural Discrete Equilibria for Shallow Water and Magnetohydrodynamic Flows*, extends the
NeurDE framework, which learns the equilibrium of a lattice Boltzmann solver with a neural network, to
the shallow water equations and magnetohydrodynamics. Along the way I implemented polynomial
equilibria, a spectral Orszag–Tang reference solver and KBC entropic stabilization, and derived an
entropically consistent KBC equilibrium via constrained minimization.

Before that I worked on cyber threat intelligence at scale: an NLP pipeline over millions of darknet
forum posts and chat messages, published at WACCO (IEEE EuroS&P Workshops) 2025. I spent eight months
as a cyber security engineer intern at Hilti, and my bachelor's thesis on multi-agent reinforcement
learning for collective behaviour was done during a research stay at Harvard University.

Most recently I built [nanosearch](https://github.com/noecaneva/nanosearch), a testbed for teaching
small open models (0.5–1.5B) to drive a search engine with reinforcement learning (GRPO). I designed
the experiments and built it with Claude Code.

I also enjoy explaining technical work: I have taught coding to children and teenagers, designed and
taught an AI and cybersecurity course at Oxford Royale Academy, and briefed journalists on AI and
cyber threats at an event hosted by Google Switzerland.

**Interests:** AI safety and adversarial ML, scientific ML and numerical methods, high-performance
computing, security research.
